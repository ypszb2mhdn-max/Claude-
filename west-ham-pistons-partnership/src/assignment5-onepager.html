Key findings:

  • Context raises the pressure. Relegation, rivalry and the media push managers towards cutting corners.
  • Individuals decide. The manager’s risk attitude, ethics and background determine how they respond.
  • Governance is the main lever. It is the factor the club controls, and it moderates all the others.

3) Recommendations and potential outcomes

  1. Financial and integrity guardrails. A wage ceiling, a plan B if promotion fails, an independent Risk & Integrity Committee, and a sponsor ethics policy. Outcome: financial stability, less misconduct, more trust.
  2. An independent player-welfare and data protocol. Only the medical staff decide on return to play, access to health data is strictly limited, and a media policy forbids disclosing medical information. Outcome: fewer re-injuries, protected privacy, sporting integrity.
  3. Leadership selection and succession. Appoint leaders on competence and ethical leadership, plan for three years instead of sacking coaches reactively, and renew the board. Outcome: stability, an ethical culture, better long-term performance.
