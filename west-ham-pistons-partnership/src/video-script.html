Video proposal script: West Ham United to the Detroit Pistons

Maximum length 5:00 · Spoken text about 560 words, roughly 4:15 to 4:30 at a calm pace · Addressed directly to the Pistons

Recording tips Speak to the camera as if the Pistons’ leadership is opening the video. Read the grey visual lines as notes for your slides or footage; they are not spoken. If several of you are presenting, split the scenes between you (for example, Speaker A for scenes 1, 2 and 6, and Speaker B for scenes 3 to 5). Time a full run-through before recording, and cut a sentence from scene 3 if you go over 4:50.
1 · Opening0:00 – 0:30
Visual · Old photos: an East London shipyard, then a piston factory · Title card: “West Ham United × Detroit Pistons”

Hello, Detroit.

In 1895, workers at a shipyard in East London started a football team. In 1941, workers at a piston factory in Fort Wayne started a basketball team. One became West Ham United. The other became the Detroit Pistons.

We’re West Ham United, and this message is for you.

2 · Why you0:30 – 1:15
Visual · On screen: “2023/24: 14–68” → “2025/26: 60–22, 1st in the East” · then “West Ham: relegated, May 2026”

We want to be honest with you. In May, after fourteen years in the Premier League, we were relegated.

Two years ago, you knew that feeling. You lost twenty-eight games in a row. Then you rebuilt. Last season you won sixty games and finished first in the East. You’ve already done what we now have to do.

And we share something deeper. We’re both works teams from working-class cities that were knocked down and are rising again. That’s why we’re asking you, and no one else, to become our partner.

3 · Our proposal: three pillars1:15 – 3:00
Visual · Three-pillar graphic: Comeback Cities · Rebuild Lab · Two Cities Rising

We’re not looking for a sponsor, and we’re not offering you a logo deal. No money changes hands for rights. We’re proposing a partnership where each of us brings what the other doesn’t have.

Pillar one is the heart of it: Comeback Cities. A Pistons Day at our 62,500-seat London Stadium. A West Ham Night at Little Caesars Arena. Watch parties in both cities, and a documentary series, Works Teams, that tells our two stories together.

Pillar two is the Rebuild Lab. Our performance, medical and analytics staff spend time with yours, and yours with ours, sharing methods on load management, injury prevention and recovery. We want to learn how you reset your culture and your decision-making. In return, we’ll open up the Academy of Football, the academy that produced Bobby Moore, Frank Lampard and Declan Rice, to your development programme.

Pillar three is Two Cities Rising. Our Foundation and your community team run one youth programme in Newham and in Detroit, and every year young people travel between the two cities.

So what’s in it for you? London. The NBA is planning a league in Europe, with London as a key market. We can give you a credible home here: a 130-year-old club with a global fanbase, and supporters who will trust you because we introduced you.

4 · What it delivers and how we measure it3:00 – 3:45
Visual · KPI tiles: UK fans +50% · US fans +40% · Injury days −10% · 2,000 young people · 15m content views

What will this deliver? For you: a growing fanbase in the UK and Europe, a London presence before NBA Europe tips off, and new stories to tell. For us: supporters who stay close through a hard season, fewer days lost to injury, a faster rebuild, and new fans in the US. And together: two thousand young people reached by year three.

We’ll measure all of it: fan growth in each other’s markets, injury days, community reach, and an annual survey on how fair the partnership feels to both sides.

5 · How we’ll run it3:45 – 4:25
Visual · Timeline: Dec 2026 talks → Mar 2027 agreement → Apr 2027 launch → Mar 2028 review → Dec 2029 renew

We’ll keep it simple. A steering group with three people from each club, meeting every quarter. One partnership manager on each side. Three years, starting in April 2027, with an honest review after year one, and either of us free to walk away if it isn’t working. Each club funds its own staff, and joint projects are split fifty-fifty.

6 · Close4:25 – 5:00
Visual · Both club names · Tagline: “Two works teams. Two cities. Two comebacks.”

Detroit, you showed the world how to come back. Let’s show it what two comeback clubs can build together: something neither of us could build alone.

We’d love to meet your leadership team in December to talk it through.

Two works teams. Two cities. Two comebacks. Let’s get to work.
